A FEW GOOD QUESTIONS

Let’s clear
things up.

Do we need to replace our software?

We start with the tools you already use. Where integration is available, we connect the workflow. Where it isn’t, we agree a practical handoff before promising a connection.

Can we start with one small task?

Yes. The first step is a focused pilot with a clear scope. We agree what it should handle, where your team takes over and how we’ll review the results.

Will Feleo make decisions for our team?

The boundaries are agreed with you. Medical judgements, tax advice and exceptional requests stay with qualified people. Feleo supports the routine work around them.

What happens after an enquiry?

We discuss your business, the task you want help with and your existing systems. From there, we propose a specific starting point and the work involved.
