# Design history

The initial private prototype explored Fraunces and Instrument Serif with Hanken body copy. On 2026-10-17 Aleksa selected the **Hanken-only** view. Current rules are in docs/BRAND.md; do not restore the earlier serif direction as a default.

The initial atmospheric background was created with the built-in image generation tool. Brief: bright pale-blue morning sky above distant alpine mountains and cloud, small wildflower slope, quiet editorial atmosphere, no people, text or UI.

The feather SVG is a prototype reconstruction of the approved two-part symbol. The final brand kit remains on the roadmap.
